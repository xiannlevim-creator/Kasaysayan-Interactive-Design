Quest: Hearts and Minds (1901)

The Thomasites and public education

Following the pacification of the archipelago, the United States deploys education as a strategic tool to win over the Filipino population and introduce American governance, culture, and ideals.

Building a school system

  1. August 21, 1901

    Arrival of the Thomasites

    Over 500 American teachers arrive in Manila aboard the USS Thomas to establish public schools and train local educators across the provinces.

  2. Act No. 74

    Free Public Education

    Act No. 74 establishes the Department of Public Instruction, making primary education free and accessible to all Filipinos regardless of social class.

  3. New Curriculum

    English as the Medium

    English becomes the official language of instruction across all school levels to unify communication and accelerate the adoption of the new curriculum.

  4. 1901 and 1908

    Founding of Institutions

    New higher learning centers are established, including the Philippine Normal School (now PNU) in 1901 for teacher training and the University of the Philippines (UP) in 1908.
