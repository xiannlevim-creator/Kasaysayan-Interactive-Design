Japan Quest · Phase 2

Quest: Shadows of the Second Republic (1942–1945)

The wartime puppet government · 1942–1945

Following the fall of Allied defenses, Japan tightens its grip on the Philippines by establishing a puppet government to project an illusion of independence.

Key Events

  • October 14, 1943

    The Puppet State

    On October 14, 1943, Japan inaugurates the Second Philippine Republic with José P. Laurel as President, enforcing strict political control through the state-sponsored KALIBAPI party.

  • 1942–1945

    Mickey Mouse Money

    Japanese-issued fiat currency triggers extreme hyperinflation and severe food shortages, forcing locals to rely on black-market bartering to survive.

  • 1942–1945

    Guerrilla Underground

    Refusing to recognize the regime, resistance fighters like the Hukbalahap wage relentless sabotage and espionage from the countryside.

  • August 17, 1945

    End of the Regime

    As Allied forces liberate the islands, Laurel flees to Japan and formally dissolves the puppet government on August 17, 1945, following Japan's surrender.
