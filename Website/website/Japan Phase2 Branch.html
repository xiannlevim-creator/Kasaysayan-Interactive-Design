Quest: The Puppet President (1943–1945)

José P. Laurel is appointed by Imperial Japan to lead the Second Philippine Republic, serving as a buffer between the occupying army and the Filipino people.

  • The Appointment

    Left behind by President Quezon to protect civilians, Laurel becomes president of the Japanese-sponsored puppet government on October 14, 1943.

  • Limited Power

    Despite holding the highest office, Laurel operates under strict Japanese military surveillance with zero real autonomy.

  • Silent Defiance

    He successfully blocks Japanese demands to conscript Filipinos into fighting against the Allies, surviving multiple guerrilla assassination attempts in the process.

  • Forced Compliance

    Under intense pressure from Tokyo, Laurel declares a state of war against the United States and Great Britain in September 1944, though he refuses to enforce a military draft.

  • The Dissolution

    Retreating to Japan as Allied forces liberate the islands, Laurel formally dissolves the puppet regime on August 17, 1945, and is later granted amnesty from treason charges.
