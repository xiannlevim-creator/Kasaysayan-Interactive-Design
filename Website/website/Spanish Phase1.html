Spanish Quest · Phase 1

Quest: The Cross and the Sword (1521)

Magellan's expedition · First contact

In search of a western route to the Spice Islands, a Spanish expedition led by Ferdinand Magellan makes first European contact with the archipelago, setting the stage for future colonization.

Key Events

  1. March 16, 1521

    First Landing

    Magellan's fleet sights the mountains of Samar and drops anchor at Homonhon Island, making initial contact with local islanders.

  2. 1521

    The Blood Compact

    Magellan forms a traditional sandugo (blood compact) with Rajah Kulambu of Limasawa and later Rajah Humabon of Cebu, forging early alliances.

  3. March 31, 1521

    The First Mass and Baptism

    The first Catholic Mass is celebrated in Limasawa, followed shortly by the mass baptism of Humabon and hundreds of Cebuanos.

  4. April 27, 1521

    Battle of Mactan

    Local chieftain Lapulapu and his warriors defeat the Spanish forces on the shores of Mactan, killing Magellan and forcing the surviving crew to flee.
