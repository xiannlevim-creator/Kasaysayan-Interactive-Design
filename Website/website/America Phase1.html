Quest: The Stars and Stripes Arrive (1898)

At the height of the Spanish-American War, the United States intervenes in the Philippine Revolution, bringing an end to over three centuries of Spanish rule and sparking a new struggle for sovereignty.

Key Events

  1. May 1, 1898

    Battle of Manila Bay

    Led by Commodore George Dewey, the U.S. Asiatic Squadron steams into Manila Bay and destroys the Spanish Pacific fleet in a matter of hours.

  2. June–July 1898

    The Ground Surge

    U.S. expeditionary forces begin landing in Cavite and Parañaque throughout June and July, forming a tense, temporary alliance with Emilio Aguinaldo's revolutionary army.

  3. August 13, 1898

    The Mock Battle of Manila

    Following a secret agreement, Spanish forces surrender the walled city of Intramuros to American troops after a staged skirmish, deliberately locking Filipino fighters out.

  4. December 10, 1898

    The Treaty of Paris

    Spain officially cedes the archipelago to the United States for $20 million, setting the stage for the Philippine-American War.
