Quest: The Promise of Independence (1916)

The Jones Law · Philippine Autonomy Act

As Filipino leaders push for greater autonomy, the United States passes the Jones Law (Philippine Autonomy Act), formally pledging to grant full independence once a stable government is established.

A promise with conditions

  1. Jones Law · 1916

    The Bicameral Legislature

    The American-dominated Philippine Commission is replaced by an all-Filipino legislature with an elected Senate and House of Representatives.

  2. Manuel L. Quezon

    A National Hero's Return

    After championing the bill in Washington as Resident Commissioner, Quezon returns to Manila and becomes the first President of the Philippine Senate.

  3. Executive Authority

    Power Still Held by the U.S.

    Filipinos gain control of the legislature, but executive authority remains with an American Governor-General appointed by the U.S. President.

  4. Conditional Pledge

    Independence Promised

    For the first time in official U.S. law, the preamble promises Philippine sovereignty once a stable government is established, but sets no exact timeline.
