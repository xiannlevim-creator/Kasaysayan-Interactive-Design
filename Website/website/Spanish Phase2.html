Spanish Quest · Phase 2

Quest: The Conquistador's Foothold (1565)

Legazpi's expedition · Permanent settlement

Forty-four years after Magellan's demise, King Philip II sends Miguel López de Legazpi to establish a permanent Spanish settlement and claim the islands for the Crown.

Key Events

  1. March 16, 1565

    Blood Compact in Bohol

    Legazpi arrives in the Visayas and performs a sandugo with Boholano chieftain Datu Sikatuna, securing peace and supplies.

  2. 1565

    Fall of Cebu

    After facing resistance from Rajah Tupas, Spanish forces capture Cebu and establish Villa del Santísimo Nombre de Jesús, the first permanent Spanish settlement.

  3. 1571

    Conquest of Manila

    Legazpi's forces defeat Rajah Sulayman in Maynila, proclaiming the fortified city of Intramuros as the colonial capital of the Spanish East Indies.

  4. Across the Pacific

    The Galleon Trade

    Legazpi's navigator, Andrés de Urdaneta, discovers the return route across the Pacific to Mexico, launching the Manila-Acapulco Galleon Trade.
