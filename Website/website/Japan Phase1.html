Quest: The Rising Sun Arrives (December 1941)

The invasion of the Philippines · 1941–1942

Hours after the attack on Pearl Harbor, Imperial Japanese forces launch a surprise invasion of the Philippines on December 8, 1941.

Key Events

  1. December 1941

    Air Raids

    Japanese planes destroy Allied air defenses at Clark Field and bomb key targets across Luzon and Mindanao.

  2. December 1941

    The Pincer Invasion

    Led by Lt. Gen. Masaharu Homma, troops storm ashore at Lingayen Gulf, Aparri, Vigan, and Lamon Bay, closing in on the capital from both the north and south.

  3. January 2, 1942

    Fall of Manila

    Declared an "Open City" to prevent its destruction, Manila is occupied without a fight on January 2, 1942.

  4. 1942

    The Last Stand

    Outnumbered Filipino and American forces retreat to the Bataan Peninsula and Corregidor to mount a desperate defense.
