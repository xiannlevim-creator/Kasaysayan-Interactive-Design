Quest: The Spark of Revolution (1896)

The Katipunan · Philippine Revolution

After over three centuries of colonial rule, forced labor, and execution of reformists, the secret society Katipunan launches an armed uprising for Philippine independence.

Key Events

  1. August 19, 1896

    Discovery of the Katipunan

    Spanish authorities uncover the secret revolutionary society, forcing its Supremo, Andres Bonifacio, to accelerate plans for revolt.

  2. Late August 1896

    Cry of Pugad Lawin

    Bonifacio gathers Katipuneros in Caloocan, where they tear up their cedulas (tax certificates) to signal the start of the revolution.

  3. August 30, 1896

    Battle of Pinaglabanan

    Katipuneros attack a Spanish powder magazine in San Juan del Monte, prompting Governor-General Ramón Blanco to declare martial law across eight provinces.

  4. December 30, 1896

    Martyrdom at Bagumbayan

    Spanish authorities execute reformist writer José Rizal by firing squad, further uniting and radicalizing Filipinos against Spain.
